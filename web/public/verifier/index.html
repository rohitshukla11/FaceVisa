What is checked against (change only if you know why)

How it decides. (1) The receipt for this file's hash must be owned by FaceVisa's published receipt address. (2) Its licence must be owned by the creator address the receipt names: the creator's own entity, which only they can revoke. (3) A revocation record from that same creator means "Revoked on <date>", and outlives the licence. (4) Otherwise the licence's status and expiry say whether it's in force. Arkiv deletes an entity when it expires, so a receipt whose licence is gone shows the licence's end date, recorded on the receipt. (5) A payment in USDT on Whitechain is checked on Whitechain's public RPC: the receipt's transaction succeeded, and FaceVisa's settlement contract emitted LicensePaid for this file and licence, to the creator the receipt names, for the licence's price (and from its brand while the licence remains). An older payment in USDC over x402 on Hedera is checked on Hedera's public mirror node.

Rate limit. This uses Arkiv's public endpoint without an access key, which is rate-limited per IP address. On 4 Nov 2026 it answered RateLimit: limit=2000, reset=10 (units per 10 s); a query costs 10 units, and one check makes 5 requests. If you hit the limit, wait a few seconds.
