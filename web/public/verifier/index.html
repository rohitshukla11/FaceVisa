How this is checked

The receipt for this file's hash must come from FaceVisa's published receipt address, and its licence must be owned by the creator the receipt names. A revocation record from that creator means "Revoked on <date>"; otherwise the licence's status and end date decide. The payment is checked on Whitechain: LicensePaid from FaceVisa's settlement contract for this file, licence, creator and price.

Arkiv's public endpoint is rate-limited per address; if a check fails, wait a few seconds. This page needs no FaceVisa server and works opened from disk.
