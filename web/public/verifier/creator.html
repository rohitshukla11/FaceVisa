Where is my face used

Every ad of a creator's face that FaceVisa released, with its licence: in force, revoked or expired. This page asks Arkiv directly, from your browser, and Whitechain's RPC for the payments (Hedera's mirror node for older ones). It doesn't call FaceVisa and needs no account or access key.

What is checked against (change only if you know why)

How it decides. (1) Only receipts owned by FaceVisa's published receipt address count; receipts that name the creator from any other address are ignored and counted. (2) Each receipt's licence must be owned by the creator's own key and be for that exact file. (3) A revocation record from the creator's key means "Revoked on <date>", and outlives the licence. (4) Otherwise the licence's status and expiry say whether it's in force; when Arkiv has deleted an expired licence, the end recorded on the receipt is shown. (5) Each payment is checked on chain: on Whitechain, LicensePaid from FaceVisa's settlement contract for that file, creator and price; an older x402 payment, on Hedera's mirror node. Each file links to its full check.

What it can't show. Only ads made and released through FaceVisa have receipts. A copy someone made elsewhere, without a licence, isn't on Arkiv at all.

Source: web/public/verifier/ in the FaceVisa repository. It also works opened straight from disk.
